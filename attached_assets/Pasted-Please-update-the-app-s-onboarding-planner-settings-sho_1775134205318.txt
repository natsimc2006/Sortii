Please update the app’s onboarding, planner, settings, shopping list, toasts, confirmations, and microcopy so the product feels clearly branded as Sortii: clean, fast, cheeky, and helpful.

IMPORTANT:
This is not a corporate meal planner and not a soft fluffy wellness app.
The tone should be:
- short
- confident
- light
- slightly cheeky
- practical
- never cringey
- never over-written

The app should feel like:
“Dinner stress removed.”
“Set a few basics — we’ll handle the rest.”
“Fast, smart, useful.”

11. KID-FRIENDLY RECIPE LOGIC
==================================================

Ensure the planner can actually find and prioritise kid-friendly meals when the Kid-Friendly Recipes preference is enabled.

Implementation guidance:
- Add recipe field/tag such as:
  kidFriendly: true/false
  or kidFriendlyScore

If missing, infer from recipe characteristics like:
- mild flavours
- low spice
- familiar ingredients
- simple presentation
- broader family appeal
- lower complexity

Kid-friendly examples:
- pasta bakes
- tacos with simple fillings
- mild chicken rice dishes
- meatballs
- tray bakes
- simple stir fries
- familiar tomato- or cheese-based meals

Avoid prioritising as kid-friendly:
- very spicy dishes
- highly bitter ingredients
- very niche flavours
- overly complex gourmet meals
- unusual textures

Planner behaviour:
- if Kid-Friendly Recipes is ON, increase ranking weight
- do not make it a total hard restriction unless necessary
- combine sensibly with budget, leftovers, shared ingredients, and recipe complexity
