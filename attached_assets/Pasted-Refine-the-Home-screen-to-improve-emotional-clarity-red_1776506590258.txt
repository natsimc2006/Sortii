Refine the Home screen to improve emotional clarity, reduce overwhelm, and guide the user toward simple next actions.

GOAL
Make the Home screen feel like:
- a calm check-in
- not a dashboard
- not overwhelming
- one clear next step

--------------------------------
1. HEADER COPY REFINEMENT
--------------------------------

Keep the greeting and reassurance tone, but tighten the wording.

Replace:
“You’re in reasonable shape. Nothing’s on fire.”

With a more natural, slightly smoother version such as:
- “You’re in good shape. Nothing’s on fire.”
OR
- “Nothing’s on fire. You’re doing alright.”

Keep tone calm, grounded, slightly human.

--------------------------------
2. DINNER CARD (ANCHOR)
--------------------------------

Keep the “Tonight’s dinner” card as the primary focus.

Minor tweak:
- Reduce emphasis on “Sorted ✓”
- Let the meal name be the visual hero

This card should remain the main anchor of the screen.

--------------------------------
3. REWORK FAMILY + MONEY SECTION
--------------------------------

Currently:
Family and Money appear as equal navigation cards.

Instead:
Turn this into a “what needs your attention” layer.

IMPLEMENT:

IF there is nothing urgent:
- Show a soft reassurance line above these cards:
  “Everything’s ticking along. Nothing you need to jump into.”

- Then show Family and Money cards below

IF something needs setup (e.g. money not set up):
- Highlight ONLY that card more prominently
- Add a subtle nudge:
  “Worth a quick look → your weekly spend”

Do not present multiple competing calls to action.

--------------------------------
4. WEEK AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------

Reduce visual dominance of this section.

- Slightly reduce contrast or weight
- Keep functionality the same
- Ensure it feels like supporting context, not the main focus

--------------------------------
5. UPCOMING MODULES (APPOINTMENTS / BILLS)
--------------------------------

Adjust tone so these feel helpful, not empty.

Replace “Soon” labels with:
- “On the way”
OR
- “Coming to Sortii”

Optional supporting copy:
- “We’ll help with this soon”

--------------------------------
6. OVERALL PRIORITY
--------------------------------

Ensure the Home screen reads in this order:

1. Reassurance (you’re okay)
2. Tonight (one thing handled)
3. One gentle nudge (if needed)
4. Supporting info (week view, future tools)

Avoid presenting multiple competing actions at once.

--------------------------------
FINAL GOAL

The user should feel:
- calmer after opening the app
- not overwhelmed
- like they only need to do one small thing (if anything)

Do not redesign layout structure.
Only refine hierarchy, copy, and emphasis.
