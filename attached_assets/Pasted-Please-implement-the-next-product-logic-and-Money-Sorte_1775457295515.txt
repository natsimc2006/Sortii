Please implement the next product-logic and Money Sorted improvements for Sortii. Keep the current Sortii brand, rounded styling, teal-led palette, soft premium feel, and calm tone. Do not redesign the app. This is a logic, hierarchy, and value-communication pass.

PRIMARY GOAL
Make the budget tiers feel genuinely different, reduce redundant planner actions, and make the Money Sorted page clearly show the value Sortii creates.

⸻

	1.	MAKE BUDGET TIERS GENERATE DIFFERENT WEEKS

⸻

Current issue:
Super Budget, Budget, and Standard are returning very similar meals and costs. This makes the budget selector feel cosmetic and weak. Only Premium currently feels meaningfully different.

Please update the meal-generation logic so each budget tier acts as a distinct meal-planning profile, not just a label.

Budget tier behaviour

Super Budget
Meal profile:
	•	lowest-cost meals
	•	pantry-friendly meals
	•	batch-friendly meals
	•	simpler ingredient lists
	•	more overlap in ingredients across the week
	•	more practical family basics
	•	fewer premium proteins
	•	fewer speciality ingredients

Generation priorities:
	1.	lowest cost
	2.	pantry overlap
	3.	batch value
	4.	simplicity
	5.	variety

Budget
Meal profile:
	•	still cost-conscious
	•	practical, everyday meals
	•	slightly more variety than Super Budget
	•	moderate ingredient count
	•	balanced low-cost proteins
	•	simple prep

Generation priorities:
	1.	cost
	2.	practicality
	3.	prep ease
	4.	balanced variety

Standard
Meal profile:
	•	more balanced and varied
	•	broader mix of meals
	•	more freshness and flexibility
	•	less repetition than Super Budget/Budget
	•	still sensible, but not tightly cost-led

Generation priorities:
	1.	variety
	2.	balanced cost
	3.	freshness
	4.	interest across the week

Premium
Keep Premium as the broadest and most flexible tier:
	•	more indulgent meals
	•	more premium proteins
	•	more variety
	•	less cost sensitivity
	•	greater ingredient tolerance

Generation priorities:
	1.	variety
	2.	indulgence
	3.	premium ingredients
	4.	lower cost sensitivity
