PART 3 — Update suggestion display rules

Update which suggestions show and when:

When weeklyState is 'over' or 'tight':
  Show up to 3 suggestions.
  Priority order:
    1. Any personalised suggestions (streaming, coffee, entertainment)
       if their conditions are met — these always come first.
    2. eating_out_swap if condition met.
    3. swap_takeaway.
    4. fridge_raid.
    5. batch_meal or leftover_night if condition met.

When weeklyState is 'ok', 'good', or 'great':
  Show up to 2 suggestions.
  Priority order:
    1. leftover_night if condition met.
    2. freezer_check.

Never show a suggestion if its condition is not met.
