Refine the existing Sortii app with a focus on:
1) Making Home the clear, consistent entry point
2) Simplifying and improving the “Help me sort this” experience
3) Tightening tone and reducing UI/mental noise

IMPORTANT:
This is a refinement pass, not a rebuild.
Do NOT redesign the app or change the core structure.
Preserve the current visual style, spacing, and component system.

---

1. FIX APP ENTRY BEHAVIOUR (CRITICAL)

Current issue:
App reopens on last active tab (e.g. Meals).

Required behaviour:
- App must ALWAYS open on Home
- Ignore previously active tab on launch

Implementation guidance:
- Disable navigation state persistence OR
- Force initial route to Home on app mount
- Ensure tab navigation still works normally within a session

Goal:
Home becomes the emotional entry point every time the app opens.

---

2. HOME SCREEN — REFINEMENT

Keep the current structure, but improve clarity and tone.

A. Weekly summary (top line)

Keep style, refine tone slightly.

Good examples:
- “You’re in reasonable shape. Nothing’s on fire.”
- “A fairly steady week. No surprises lurking.”

Avoid:
- overly long sentences
- anything motivational or preachy

---

B. Rebalance content hierarchy

Ensure these feel like equal life areas:
- Tonight’s dinner
- Family
- Money

Do NOT visually prioritise meals too heavily.

---

C. Money card tone improvement

Replace:
“See how your week looks financially.”

With something more human:

Examples:
- “Curious how this week looks financially?”
- “Want a quick look at the money side?”
- “There’s some breathing room here if you want it.”

Keep tone:
light, optional, non-pressured

---

D. Week at a glance

Keep current UI.

Prepare for future support of mixed signals:
(meals, family, appointments, bills)

No major redesign required yet.

---

3. “HELP ME SORT THIS” — SIMPLIFY (VERY IMPORTANT)

This feature must reduce overwhelm, not summarise the week.

Current issue:
Too many completed items → feels like a checklist.

---

A. Reduce content

Show:
- MAX 1–2 items only

Example:

Header:
“Alright. Let’s keep this simple.”

Then:

Option 1:
“Tonight’s dinner is sorted.”

Optional second:
“Tomorrow’s looking fine.”

Then:

Final line:
“That’ll do.”

---

B. Remove instructional UI

Remove:
- “Tap one to acknowledge it”
- any system-style instructions

Replace with:
- no instruction OR
- soft line like “You’re good.”

---

C. Interaction behaviour

- Tapping an item can lightly acknowledge it
- No heavy navigation
- No expanding into full screens
- Keep everything contained

---

D. Emotional goal

User should feel:
“I don’t need to do everything right now.”

NOT:
“Here’s everything that’s done / not done”

---

4. MEALS PAGE — MICROCOPY TWEAK

Refine header:

Current:
“7 dinners planned. Dinner’s cooperating this week.”

Update to:
“7 dinners sorted. Dinner’s behaving itself.”

Keep tone:
dry, observational, light

---

5. FAMILY MODULE — VISUAL CLEANUP

Reduce visual clutter slightly.

- Keep “Kids & activities” expanded
- Collapse:
  - pickups & dropoffs
  - weekend plans
  - family notes

Goal:
Less scanning, more clarity

---

6. GENERAL TONE GUARDRAILS

Across the app, ensure:

DO:
- keep tone warm and slightly wry
- use observational language
- keep things short and natural

DO NOT:
- use productivity language
- use motivational phrases
- use instructions unless necessary
- over-explain

---

7. OVERALL UX GOAL

After refinement, the app should feel like:

- a calm weekly snapshot
- not a planner demanding attention
- not a checklist system
- not something that creates pressure

The Home screen should make the user think:

“Okay. That’s manageable.”

And the “Help me sort this” flow should feel like:

“I only need to do one small thing. Or nothing.”

---

IMPORTANT FINAL RULE

If a change adds complexity, remove it.

Sortii should always trend toward:
less noise, fewer decisions, softer interactions.
