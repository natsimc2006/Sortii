Enhance my Sortii mobile app with a subtle “delight layer” using micro-interactions, motion, and feedback.

IMPORTANT:
- Keep all animations fast, subtle, and purposeful
- Do NOT add heavy or distracting animations
- Do NOT slow down the app
- Focus on polish, responsiveness, and small moments of satisfaction

--------------------------------------------------
1. BUTTON INTERACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Apply to all buttons:
- On press: scale down slightly (0.97)
- On release: return to normal (1.0) with soft easing
- Keep transitions quick (100–150ms)

Gold CTA:
- Slight shadow increase or glow on press

--------------------------------------------------
2. CARD INTERACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Meal cards and tappable cards:
- On tap: slight lift (scale 1 → 1.02)
- Smooth transition into detail screens

--------------------------------------------------
3. SWAP INTERACTION
--------------------------------------------------

When a meal is swapped:
- Animate card transition (fade or slide)
- Slight lift effect during change
- Show temporary micro-label:
  “Saved $X” or “Smarter choice”

--------------------------------------------------
4. COMPLETION MOMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

When week is fully planned:
- Animate checkmark appearing
- Fade in confirmation text
- Optional soft haptic feedback

--------------------------------------------------
5. SHOPPING LIST INTERACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

When ticking items:
- Animate checkbox fill
- Fade or strike through item
- Slight row fade

When section complete:
- Show subtle “Done” indicator

--------------------------------------------------
6. LOADING / AUTO ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

For “Fill gaps” or auto planning:
- Show short loading shimmer (300–500ms)
- Animate items appearing in quick sequence

--------------------------------------------------
7. IMAGE LOADING
--------------------------------------------------

Recipe images:
- Fade in smoothly
- Optional very subtle zoom effect on load

--------------------------------------------------
8. TRANSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Use:
- smooth ease-in-out transitions
- short durations (100–300ms)
- consistent motion across app

--------------------------------------------------
9. MICROCOPY (OPTIONAL)
--------------------------------------------------

Use short, subtle phrases:
- “Sorted.”
- “Done.”
- “Saved you $X.”
- “That was easy.”

Keep tone:
- calm
- helpful
- slightly cheeky

--------------------------------------------------
10. FINAL GOAL
--------------------------------------------------

The app should feel:
- responsive
- satisfying
- polished
- effortless to use

Not flashy — just quietly excel
