## SORTII — Connected intelligence layer: Money states, Bills, 
## Appointments, Family Juggle + Home screen synthesis

This is an architectural pass connecting the Bills, Appointments, 
Family, and Money modules into a unified weekly output. Work through 
each phase in order. Stop and confirm between phases.

---

## PHASE 1 — Bills: add Regular / One-off toggle

On the bill entry screen (Add bill flow), add a type selector 
at the top of the form.

Selector: "What kind of expense is this?"

Option 1: "Regular bill"
- Teal pill when selected (#2A8F88 background, white text)
- Subtext: "Happens every month — rent, utilities, subscriptions."
- Bills tagged as Regular feed into the monthly budget baseline only.
  They do not surface as alerts on the Home screen or Money tab
  (they are already accounted for in the budget setup).

Option 2: "One-off expense"  
- Gold pill when selected (#D29A22 background, white text)
- Subtext: "Outside your normal budget — rego, school fees, 
  repairs, anything unexpected."
- Bills tagged as One-off are treated as irregular costs and 
  surface as pressure signals in the Money tab and Home screen.

Store this as billType: 'regular' | 'oneoff' on each bill record.

One-off bills also require a due date — make this field required 
when One-off is selected, optional for Regular.

Display in the Bills list:
- Regular bills: show with a teal dot indicator
- One-off expenses: show with a gold dot indicator
- Sort: One-off expenses due soonest appear at the top

---

## PHASE 2 — Appointments: add cost and availability flags

On the appointment entry screen (Add appointment flow), add two 
optional flags below the standard fields (title, date, time, who):

FLAG 1: "Does this have a cost?"
- Toggle: off by default
- When toggled on: show a currency input field
  Label: "Estimated cost"
  Placeholder: $0
  Frequency: one-off (no frequency selector needed)
- Store as: appointmentCost: number | null

FLAG 2: "Does this affect who's available?"
- Toggle: off by default  
- When toggled on: show two fields:
  Field 1: "Who's unavailable?" 
  — shows family member selector (pulls from Family members list)
  — multi-select: can flag multiple people
  Field 2: "For how long?"
  — options: "This appointment only" | "All day" | "Custom hours"
  — if Custom hours: show time range picker
- Store as: availabilityBlock: { 
    personIds: string[], 
    scope: 'appointment' | 'allday' | 'custom',
    startTime?: string,
    endTime?: string
  } | null

These flags are optional — most appointments won't need them.
Show them collapsed under a "More details" expand link to avoid
overwhelming the entry form.

## PHASE 3 — Money tab: three rendered states

Replace the current single Money tab layout with three distinct 
rendered states. The correct state is determined at render time 
from the user's financial data.

### State determination logic:

Calculate weeklyPressureScore:

Start with weeklyBreathingRoom (from existing budget calculation).

Then add pressure from:
- Any one-off bills due within the next 7 days: 
  add their cost to this week's outgoings
- Any appointments with cost flag due within the next 7 days:
  add appointmentCost to this week's outgoings

Recalculate:
adjustedBreathingRoom = weeklyBreathingRoom 
  - sum(oneOffBillsDueThisWeek) 
  - sum(appointmentCostsDueThisWeek)

State rules:
- adjustedBreathingRoom > 50: STATE 1 (calm)
- adjustedBreathingRoom 1–50: STATE 2 (tight)
- adjustedBreathingRoom <= 0: STATE 3 (stretched)
- If no budget has been set up yet: show setup prompt (existing)

---

### STATE 1 — Calm week

Background: white. Minimal layout. No hero card needed.

Show:
- Large teal checkmark icon
- Heading (20px, bold): "This week looks fine."
- Subtext (14px, grey): "Nothing unexpected coming. 
  Dinner's already covered."
- If weeklyBreathingRoom > 0: small teal line:
  "~$[adjustedBreathingRoom] breathing room this week."
- If no one-off bills or appointment costs this week: 
  show nothing else. Silence is the message.

Do NOT show: budget breakdown, quick wins, savings goal card 
in this state — save those for when they're relevant.

At the bottom: small grey text link "See full picture →" 
which expands to the full budget breakdown for users who want it.

---

### STATE 2 — Tight week

Show:
- Gold/amber warning icon (use #D29A22)
- Heading (20px, bold): "Tighter than usual this week."
- List of pressure items — render each one-off bill or 
  appointment cost due this week as a line item:
  Format: "· [Name] — $[amount] due [day]"
  Example: "· Car rego — $189 due Thursday"
  Example: "· Dental — $180 due Friday"
  Line item text: #2B2B2B, 14px
- Gap line (1px, #E6E1D7)
- Teal line: "Dinner plan saves ~$[mealPlanSaving] vs eating out. 
  That helps."
- Final line in grey: 
  "Breathing room this week: ~$[adjustedBreathingRoom]"
  If adjustedBreathingRoom < 20: render this in gold (#D29A22)

Below the main card:
- Collapsible "The quick version" (existing budget snapshot)
- "A few quick wins" section (existing, if tips available)
- Savings goal card (existing, if goal is set)

---

### STATE 3 — Stretched week

Tone shifts. Layout is quieter, more focused.

Show:
- No icons, no colour blocks — white background, calm
- Heading (22px, bold): "This week is stretched."
- Subtext (14px, grey, italic): 
  "Here's what's hitting all at once."
- List of ALL pressure items this week (one-off bills + 
  appointment costs + any overdue bills):
  Format: "· [Name] — $[amount] — [day]"
  Max 4 items. If more than 4 exist, show top 4 by date 
  and add "· +[n] more" as final line in grey.
- Gap line
- Single reassurance line in teal:
  "Dinner is already sorted — that's one less thing."
- Final line, small, grey:
  "Breathing room: −$[abs(adjustedBreathingRoom)] over 
  — tighter than usual."
  Render the amount in red (#B86557)

At the bottom — the overwhelm mode entry point:
- Teal text link (not a button): 
  "Want to see what can wait? →"
- This triggers Overwhelm Mode (Phase 4)

Do NOT show: quick wins, savings goal, budget breakdown 
in State 3. This is not the moment for suggestions.

---

## PHASE 4 — Overwhelm mode

Overwhelm mode is a full-screen overlay triggered by:
- Tapping "Want to see what can wait? →" in State 3
- Tapping "Help me sort this" button on Home screen

### Layout

White background. No nav bar visible. No cards. Calm.

Top: small teal text, centred, 12px:
"FOCUS MODE"

Heading (24px, bold, centred):
"Right now, just focus on this."

Then render a maximum of 3 items, selected by this priority:
1. Any bill or one-off expense due today or tomorrow (most urgent)
2. Any appointment today or tomorrow
3. One thing that is already handled — the highest-priority item 
   from Meals (tonight's dinner) or Family (sorted events)

Always include at least one "already handled" item. 
The user needs to feel the ground.

Render each item as a simple row:
- Icon: ⚠️ for urgent money/bill items, 📅 for appointments, 
  ✅ for handled items
- Text: one line only, plain language
  Examples:
  "Rego payment — $189 due Thursday"
  "Dental — Friday 2pm"
  "✅ Dinner tonight — Lamb Stew. Sorted."

Below the 3 items:
- Grey line (1px)
- Small grey text, centred, 13px: "Everything else can wait."

Bottom of screen (not a nav bar — just two text links, centred):
"I'm back  →" — exits overwhelm mode, returns to Home
"See everything →" — exits overwhelm mode, goes to Home 
  with all cards visible

### Tone rules for overwhelm mode
- No dollar totals unless actionable right now
- No suggestions, no "you could save X"
- No comparisons
- Short sentences only
- Never more than 3 items, ever
- Always end on something that is handled

## PHASE 5 — Home screen synthesis

The Home screen "What's Coming Up" card and the Money card 
must now draw from all connected data layers.

### Money card on Home screen

Pull from the Money tab state (Phase 3) and render one line:

State 1: "Money looks fine this week." — grey text, no icon
State 2: "Tighter than usual — [top pressure item] coming up." 
  — gold left border, teal "Set it up →" if no budget set
State 3: "Stretched week. [count] costs landing." 
  — red left border (#B86557)
No budget set: existing "Curious how this week looks?" card

### What's Coming Up card on Home screen

This card should synthesise across all modules. 

Pull and prioritise in this order:
1. One-off bills due this week or next (gold indicator)
2. Appointments with cost flag this week (gold indicator)  
3. Appointments with availability block (teal indicator — 
   logistics signal, not financial)
4. Family juggle conflicts already detected (teal indicator)
5. Meals coverage summary (last item, always reassuring)

Render as a simple list, max 4 items:
"· Car rego — $189 — Thursday"
"· Dental — Friday 2pm (Mum)"  
"· Mum unavailable Thursday — school run needs cover"
"· ✅ 7 dinners sorted this week"

If only meals are planned and nothing else is flagged:
"A quiet one. 7 dinners sorted. Not much to worry about."

### Family Juggle conflict detection

When rendering the Family Juggle for the current week:

For each event that has an assigned responsible person:
- Check if that person has an availability block that overlaps 
  with the event time
- If overlap found: flag as a conflict

Conflict display on Family screen:
- Show a red tint (#F7E9E8) warning card above the event:
  "⚠️ [Person] is unavailable at this time — needs cover."
- On Home screen What's Coming Up card: include as a line item
  with the format:
  "[Person] unavailable [day] — [event] needs cover"

This covers the "Mum's away for work, can't do the school run" 
scenario automatically once her work trip is entered as an 
appointment with availability block flagged.

### Appointment reminders on Home screen

Appointments due in the next 7 days surface on Home screen if:
- They have a cost flag (financial awareness)
- They have an availability block (logistics awareness)
- They are within 48 hours (time urgency)

Standard appointments with no flags and more than 48 hours away 
do not surface on the Home screen — they live in Appointments 
tab only. Silence for low-priority items is correct behaviour.
