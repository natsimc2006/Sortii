Refine and improve the current Sortii app to strengthen the Home dashboard, unify the product feel, and ensure the experience remains calm, supportive, and slightly wry.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT
This app has already been refactored from a meal planner into a broader Sortii app with:
- Home (dashboard)
- Meals (module)
- Family (module)
- Add (quick add)
- More (supporting modules)

The current structure is correct.
Do NOT redesign from scratch.
This is a refinement and tightening pass.

The goal now is:
- make Home feel like a true “your week” view (not meal-dominant)
- strengthen emotional usability
- keep the warm, slightly cheeky Sortii voice consistent
- improve clarity without adding complexity

CORE PRODUCT FEEL
Sortii should feel like:
- calm
- lightly humorous
- quietly competent
- never overwhelming
- never preachy
- never overly “wellness”

Think:
“helpful friend who’s seen it all before and isn’t fussed”

---

HOME SCREEN — REBALANCE (MOST IMPORTANT)

The Home screen is currently slightly too meal-dominant.

Refine it to a **hybrid weekly overview (option C)**:

STRUCTURE:

1. Header
- Keep Sortii logo
- Keep time-based greeting

2. Add a short weekly summary line FIRST
Examples:
- “A fairly steady week. Dinner’s sorted, and not much chaos elsewhere.”
- “A couple of moving parts, but all quite manageable.”
- “You’ve got a bit on, but nothing dramatic.”

This should feel like a quick emotional read of the week.

3. Mixed “This week” signals (equal weight)

Show 3–4 cards with equal importance:

- Tonight’s dinner
- Family (next activity OR empty state)
- Breathing room (money)
- Optional future: appointments/bills placeholder

These should feel like:
👉 different parts of life sitting together calmly

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT visually prioritise meals above everything else.

WEEK AT A GLANCE — UPGRADE

Currently uses only meal icons.

Refine so it can support mixed signals:

- Meals → plate icon
- Family → star or similar
- Future: appointments → calendar dot
- Future: bills → small coin or marker

Even if only meals are active now, structure the system to support multiple types.
