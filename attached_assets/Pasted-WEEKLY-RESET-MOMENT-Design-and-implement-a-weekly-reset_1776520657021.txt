WEEKLY RESET MOMENT

Design and implement a weekly reset experience for Sortii that appears at the end of the week and helps users reflect, feel progress, and begin the next week with less mental load.

Goal

Create a calm, lightly cheeky weekly recap that feels like a gentle exhale, not a performance report.

⸻

1. Add a weekly summary card or modal

Title:
“This week, quietly sorted”

Display 2 to 4 recap lines based on real user activity across modules.

Examples of recap lines:

* “You freed up $X this week.”
* “Dinner was handled X nights.”
* “One takeaway never happened, which helped.”
* “Saturday looked busy, but it stayed upright.”
* “You kept on top of X appointments.”

Use only lines that are relevant to that user’s week.

⸻

2. Data inputs

Use available weekly signals such as:

* freedUpThisWeek
* mealsPlanned
* swapsApplied
* takeawayReduced
* appointmentsHandled
* busyDayDetected
* pantrySavings
* breathingRoom

Generate recap content dynamically from these.

⸻

3. Tone rules

Keep the tone:

* calm
* supportive
* observational
* slightly dry / human

Do not use:

* gamification language
* performance scoring
* goal-completion framing
* guilt or productivity language

⸻

4. Timing

Generate the recap at the end of the week.

Preferred behaviour:

* prepare on Sunday evening
* show on next app open
* keep visible until dismissed

⸻

5. Add one primary CTA only

Examples:

* Sort next week
* Start next week lighter
* Keep the calm going

Only one CTA should appear.

⸻

6. Add continuity suggestions

If user applied helpful actions this week, suggest carrying one forward into next week.

Examples:

* “Want to keep takeaway to one this week too?”
* “Want another lower-cost dinner mix this week?”
* “Keep that subscription paused for now?”

These should feel optional and helpful.

⸻

7. Visual design

Style the summary to feel soft and premium:

* white or warm cream base
* soft green / gold accents
* large savings number if relevant
* concise recap lines
* no graphs or detailed tables

This should feel like a calm weekly wrap-up, not a dashboard report.

⸻

8. Dismiss + persistence

Allow the user to:

* dismiss it
* revisit it later from a “This week” or “Weekly wrap” section if easy to implement
