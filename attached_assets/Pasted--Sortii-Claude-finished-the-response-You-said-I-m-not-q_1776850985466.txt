Onboarding rethink — this is the most important one

You're right, and this is worth getting right before launch. The meal-first flow is misaligned with at least two of your three user types. Here's how I'd think about it:

The three user entry points are essentially:

"Sort my family chaos" — the juggle layer is why they downloaded it
