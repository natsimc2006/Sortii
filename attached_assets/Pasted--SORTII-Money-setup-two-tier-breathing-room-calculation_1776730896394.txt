## SORTII — Money setup: two-tier breathing room calculation

Restructure the budget setup screen calculation and layout to show 
breathing room in two stages. This replaces the single "Weekly breathing 
room" card with two sequential calculation results.

---

### Input grouping changes

Move "Streaming & subscriptions" out of the "Lifestyle spending" section 
and into the committed/fixed section of the form, positioned after 
"Groceries (rough weekly spend)" and before the lifestyle section.

Relabel the input section headers:

Section 1 — committed spend inputs (in this order):
- Weekly income (/ week)
- Fixed weekly costs (/ week) — rent, mortgage, bills, loans
- Groceries rough weekly spend (/ week)
- Streaming & subscriptions (/ month) — change label to / month, 
  divide by 4.3 in all calculations
  Helper text below: "We'll work out your weekly share."

Section 2 — lifestyle spend inputs (in this order):
- Coffee & café (/ week)
- Takeaway (/ week) — if not already a separate field, add it, 
  separate from "eating out" — placeholder $0, optional
- Entertainment (/ month) — change label to / month, divide by 4.3
  Helper text below: "We'll work out your weekly share."

---

### Two-tier breathing room display

Replace the single "Weekly breathing room" card with two sequential 
result cards, displayed after all inputs, before the CTA button.

**Card 1 — Committed breathing room**

Background: teal tint (#E6F3F0), border #2A8F88

Label (small, uppercase, teal): "AFTER COMMITTED SPEND"

Formula:
committedBreathingRoom = weeklyIncome 
  - fixedWeeklyCosts 
  - groceries 
  - (subscriptions / 4.3)

Display: large number in teal (#2A8F88)
If positive: "$[X] left before lifestyle spend"
If negative: "−$[X] over before lifestyle spend" — number in red (#B86557)

Small grey subtext below (12px):
"Income minus fixed costs, groceries and subscriptions."

---

**Card 2 — After lifestyle spend**

Background: gold tint (#F6E6BA), border #D29A22

Label (small, uppercase, gold text): "AFTER LIFESTYLE SPEND"

Formula:
finalBreathingRoom = committedBreathingRoom 
  - coffee 
  - takeaway 
  - (entertainment / 4.3)

Display: large number
If positive: "$[X] breathing room" — number in teal (#2A8F88)
If negative: "−$[X] over" — number in red (#B86557)

Small grey subtext below (12px):
"This is what's left after everything — including coffee and takeaways."

---

### Safety rules
- Never render $0 in either card unless the calculated result is 
  exactly zero
- Never render $NaN or null — if any input is empty treat it as 0 
  in the calculation
- Both cards must update in real time as the user types into any field
- If lifestyle inputs are all empty/zero, suppress Card 2 entirely 
  and show only Card 1
