## 6. SMART SUGGESTIONS SYSTEM (CORE FEATURE)

Introduce a new “Smart Suggestions” section inside the Adjust experience.

This should appear:
- at the top of the Adjust screen (below the summary)
OR
- as a highlighted card above the options in the modal

---

### Smart Suggestion Types

#### 1. Budget-Based Suggestion

Trigger:
- If estimated weekly cost is above “Super Budget” threshold OR user is in Standard/Premium

Show:
“You’re overspending — want to save this week?”

CTA:
“Find cheaper swaps”

Behaviour:
- Opens swap suggestions or applies cost-saving adjustments

---

#### 2. Time-Based Suggestion

Trigger:
- If meals selected include many long cook times (e.g. >30–40 mins)

Show:
“Busy week? Want quicker dinners?”

CTA:
“Switch to faster meals”

Behaviour:
- Suggest or auto-apply quicker recipes

---

#### 3. Effort-Based Suggestion (optional but preferred)

Trigger:
- Too many complex meals or no batch meals

Show:
“Want to make this week easier?”

CTA:
“Add a batch meal”

---

### Design requirements
- Use soft card UI consistent with current design
- Slight highlight colour (teal or soft accent)
- Should feel helpful, not pushy

---

## 7. SMART SUGGESTION LOGIC (IMPLEMENTATION)

Create a simple rules engine:

Inputs:
- total weekly cost
- selected budget tier
- meal cook times
- meal complexity tags (quick, batch, etc.)

Outputs:
- 0–2 suggestions max at a time
- priority:
  1. budget
  2. time
  3. effort

Do NOT overload user with suggestions.
