Refine the Money module in the Sortii app to improve tone, clarity, and user experience.

IMPORTANT
Do not redesign the structure.
This is a tone, UX, and microcopy polish pass.

GOAL
The Money module should feel:
- calm
- observational
- slightly cheeky
- not like a finance app
- not like admin

--------------------------------
1. WEEKLY SNAPSHOT TONE
--------------------------------

Update top summary copy to feel like a small win, not a report.

Replace phrases like:
“$52 sorted by Sortii this week”

With softer, more human phrasing such as:
- “About $52 quietly sorted this week.”
- “~$52 back in your pocket.”
- “~$52 saved without trying too hard.”

Keep it understated and positive.

--------------------------------
2. BREAKDOWN COPY
--------------------------------

Keep breakdown structure but soften tone:

Examples:
- “Smarter ingredient swaps — $30”
- “Using what you already had — $22”

Optional subtext:
- “Nothing dramatic. Just less waste.”

--------------------------------
3. SECTION LABELS
--------------------------------

Replace overly formal labels like:
“Weekly snapshot”

With lighter options such as:
- “What’s going on”
- “The quick version”
- “If you care for details”

--------------------------------
4. USUAL SPEND SECTION
--------------------------------

Refine tone to be observational, not analytical.

Example:
- “Most of your grocery spend goes to dinners — about $228.”
- “Dinner does most of the damage — ~$228.”

--------------------------------
5. QUICK WINS BOX
--------------------------------

Rename and soften tone.

Replace:
“Tap for some quick wins this week”

With:
- “A few easy wins (if you want them)”
- “Low effort, decent payoff”

Update suggestions to feel human:

Instead of:
“Your grocery spend is on the higher side”

Use:
- “Grocery spend’s creeping up a bit.”
- “You’re not overspending… but you’re not not either.”

Instead of:
“Do a freezer audit”

Use:
- “There’s almost definitely something in the freezer.”
- “Freezer check before you shop = free food you forgot about.”

Keep savings amounts visible.

--------------------------------
6. SAVINGS GOAL BEHAVIOUR
--------------------------------

Make savings goals fully optional.

Fix logic:
- If user skips goal setup, do NOT show forced goal screen
- Continue flow normally

Update savings goal card:

Title options:
- “Saving for something?”
- “Got something in mind?”

Subtext:
- “We’ll keep an eye on it quietly.”
- “No pressure.”

CTA:
- “Set a goal”
- “Add one (optional)”

Secondary:
- “Not right now”

Style:
- lighter visual weight (e.g. dashed border or softer card)

--------------------------------
7. CTA BUTTON TONE
--------------------------------

Replace “Update my numbers” with:
- “Adjust my week”
- or “Tweak my numbers”

Make it feel less like admin.

--------------------------------
8. EMPTY STATES
--------------------------------

Replace:
“No money setup yet”

With:
- “No numbers yet.”
- “We’re guessing nothing. Fair enough.”

--------------------------------
9. FORM SCREEN TONE
--------------------------------

Update setup screen copy:

Instead of:
“Set your budget — we’ll handle the rest”

Use:
- “Give us rough numbers. We’ll do the thinking.”
- “Doesn’t need to be perfect.”

--------------------------------
10. SUCCESS / CONFIRMATION
--------------------------------

Use short, dry confirmations:
- “That’ll do.”
- “Good enough.”
- “We’ll work with that.”

--------------------------------
11. CONSISTENCY
--------------------------------

Ensure all Money pages:
- use white background
- match Home and Meals styling
- feel like one unified product

--------------------------------
FINAL RESULT
--------------------------------

The Money module should feel:
- helpful, not heavy
- human, not financial
- lightly witty, not jokey
- optional, not forced

It should feel like:
“a quiet nudge from someone who gets it”
