## SORTII — Cooking persona, smart meal selection + swap UX fix

Three connected improvements to the meal planning layer.
Work through each phase in order, confirm before moving on.

## PHASE 1 — Cooking persona: add to onboarding

Add a new onboarding step after the dietary needs question 
(Step 3) and before the plan is generated.

This becomes Step 4 of onboarding.

---

**Screen layout:**

Progress dots: update to show 4 steps total

Step label: "STEP 4"

Heading: "On a typical weeknight, what are you working with?"

Subtext: "We'll match meals to your real life — not a 
cooking show."

Three option cards (full-width, tappable, single select):

Option A:
Icon: ⚡
Label: "Whatever's quickest"
Subtext: "20 mins max. Minimal faff. Just needs to be done."
Value: 'quick'

Option B:
Icon: 🍳
Label: "Something decent"
Subtext: "30–45 mins. Not complicated. Tastes like effort."
Value: 'moderate'

Option C:
Icon: 👨‍🍳
Label: "Happy to cook"
Subtext: "I actually enjoy it. Time isn't really the issue."
Value: 'cook'

Selected state: teal border (#2A8F88), teal tint background 
(#E6F3F0), teal label text
Unselected state: white background, #E6E1D7 border

Default selection: none — user must actively choose.
If user taps "Skip for now →": default to 'quick' silently.
Store as: cookingPersona: 'quick' | 'moderate' | 'cook'

CTA button (gold, full width): "Sort my meals →"
Below: "Skip for now →" — small grey text link

---

**Base recipe pool filtering by persona:**

Apply these filters to the recipe pool on every plan generation:

cookingPersona === 'quick':
- Strongly prefer: cookTime <= 25 mins
- Include: cookTime 26–40 mins (up to 2 per week maximum)
- Suppress entirely: cookTime > 45 mins
- Prefer tags: 'quick', 'one-pan', 'batch', 'freezer-friendly'

cookingPersona === 'moderate':
- Prefer: cookTime <= 45 mins
- Include: cookTime 46–60 mins (up to 2 per week)
- Suppress: cookTime > 75 mins
- No tag preference

cookingPersona === 'cook':
- Full recipe pool, no time suppression
- No tag preference

Store persona in user profile. Apply on every plan generation,
including re-sorts. Never override the hard no's or dietary 
filters — persona filtering sits on top of those.

## PHASE 2 — Busyness score: wire family events to meal 
## complexity

On each weekly plan generation (and re-sort), calculate a 
busyness score from the current week's data before selecting 
recipes.

---

**Busyness score calculation:**

busyWeekScore = 0

Add 1 for each:
- Family event scheduled this week
- Appointment scheduled this week (any type)
- Availability conflict detected (person flagged unavailable 
  during an assigned event)
- Any one-off bill due this week (financial pressure 
  adds cognitive load)

Add 2 for each:
- Day where 3 or more events overlap for the same person
- Appointment with both cost flag AND availability block

busyWeekScore is calculated fresh on every plan generation.

---

**Complexity ceiling by busyness score:**

Apply these rules ON TOP of the cookingPersona filters.
Busyness overrides persona upward only — never downward.
(A 'cook' persona user on a manic week still gets quick meals.
A 'quick' persona user on a quiet week stays on quick meals.)

busyWeekScore >= 5 — HIGH BUSY:
- All meals this week: cookTime <= 25 mins only
- Suppress cookTime > 25 mins entirely for this week
- Automatically include at least 1 "easy night" slot 
  (see Easy Night spec below)
- Apply silently — no announcement to user

busyWeekScore 3–4 — MODERATE BUSY:
- Prefer cookTime <= 30 mins
- Maximum 1 meal per week with cookTime > 45 mins
- No easy night unless user requests

busyWeekScore 1–2 — LIGHT:
- Apply cookingPersona filters only, no additional ceiling

busyWeekScore 0 — QUIET:
- Full persona-filtered pool, no restrictions

---

**Day-level busyness:**

For each day of the week, also calculate a dayScore:

dayScore = count of events on that specific day
         + 1 if any family member has availability block 
           on that day

Apply to individual day meal selection:
dayScore >= 3: assign cookTime <= 20 mins meal for that day
dayScore 2: prefer cookTime <= 30 mins for that day
dayScore 0–1: no day-level restriction

Day-level rules apply within whatever the week-level 
ceiling already is.

---

**"Easy night" slot:**

When busyWeekScore >= 5, automatically insert one 
"Easy night" into the weekly plan.

Easy night is a first-class plan entry — not a gap.

Meal card for Easy night:
- Title: "Easy night"
- Subtitle: "Freezer, leftovers, or whatever's there."
- Cook time: shown as "0 mins"
- Cost: shown as "~$0" or suppress cost entirely
- Tags: none
- No recipe link — no instructions needed
- Card style: light grey tint background (#F5F5F5), 
  same card dimensions as regular meal cards

Easy night counts as a planned meal in all summaries 
("7 dinners sorted") but contributes $0 to grocery cost 
calculations.

Position: assign Easy night to the highest dayScore day 
of the week automatically.

---

**Invisible operation — nothing is announced:**

Do not show busyneessScore to the user.
Do not say "busy week mode activated."
Do not add any badge or label to the plan.

The only visible output is that the meals surfaced are 
appropriately quick. The intelligence is shown through 
outcomes, not explained.

Exception: if Easy night is inserted, a single quiet 
line appears below the meal plan summary on the Meals tab:
"One easy night built in — busy week sorted."
Teal text, 13px, italic. No further explanation.

## PHASE 3 — Meal card swap UX: consolidate toggle buttons

Currently meal cards show two separate icon buttons:
- A switch/swap icon (manually pick a replacement)
- A zap/lightning icon (auto-change the meal)

The zap icon's purpose is not clear to users and the 
auto-swap feature is being missed. Consolidate into a 
single "Switch meal" entry point that then offers the 
choice between auto and manual.

---

**Remove from meal cards:**

Remove the zap/lightning icon button entirely from all 
meal cards. Remove the existing separate swap icon button.

---

**Add to meal cards:**

Add a single text button to each meal card:

Label: "Switch meal"
Style: 
- Small pill button
- White background
- Teal border (#2A8F88, 1px)
- Teal text (#2A8F88)
- Font size: 12px
- Font weight: 600
- Border radius: 20px
- Padding: 4px 12px
- Position: bottom-left of meal card, replacing existing 
  toggle buttons

---

**Switch meal bottom sheet:**

When "Switch meal" is tapped, open a bottom sheet modal.

Bottom sheet header:
- Title: "Switch this meal" (16px, bold)
- Subtitle: "[Current meal name]" (13px, grey)
- Close handle at top (standard)

Two options, rendered as full-width tappable cards:

Option A — Auto pick:
Icon: ⚡
Label: "Pick one for me"
Subtext: "We'll find the best fit for your week."
Style: teal tint (#E6F3F0) background, teal border

Option B — Choose yourself:
Icon: 📋
Label: "Let me choose"
Subtext: "Browse what's available and pick your own."
Style: white background, #E6E1D7 border

---

**Option A behaviour — Auto pick:**

Immediately selects a replacement recipe using these rules:
- Must not be a recipe already in this week's plan
- Must respect cookingPersona filter
- Must respect current day's dayScore ceiling
- Must respect hard no's and dietary filters
- Prefer recipes not used in the last 2 weeks if history 
  is available

On selection: meal card updates immediately.
Brief confirmation line below the card (2 seconds, 
then fades):
"Swapped. That's better." — teal, 13px

---

**Option B behaviour — Let me choose:**

Opens the existing recipe browser/picker filtered to:
- Recipes that pass cookingPersona filter
- Recipes that pass current day's complexity ceiling
- Excludes recipes already in this week's plan
- Excludes hard no's and dietary exclusions

User selects a recipe. Meal card updates.
Same confirmation: "Swapped. That's better."

---

**Preserve all other meal card actions:**

All other existing meal card icon buttons (recipe view, 
batch indicator, hide, remove) remain exactly as they are.
Only the swap/zap buttons are affected by this change.
