7. GLOBAL MICROCOPY SYSTEM
--------------------------------

Apply consistent tone across the app.

--------------------------------
A. TONE RULES

Use:
- short sentences
- understated reassurance
- light observational humour

Avoid:
- motivational language
- corporate tone
- overly emotional phrasing

--------------------------------
B. EMPTY STATES

Examples:

Family:
- “Nothing on here yet. Enjoy the quiet.”
- “All clear. Rare, but we’ll take it.”

Pickups:
- “No one needs to be anywhere. Easy.”

Weekend:
- “Nothing locked in. Plenty of options.”

Notes:
- “Nothing written down. Living dangerously.”

--------------------------------
C. SUCCESS STATES

- “That works.”
- “All sorted. Leave it there.”
- “Holding steady.”

--------------------------------
D. ERROR STATES

Replace:
“Something went wrong”

With:
- “That didn’t work. Try again.”
- “Bit of a hiccup. Try again.”

--------------------------------
E. BUTTON TEXT

Replace generic labels:

Continue → “That’ll do”
Save → “Keep it”
Cancel → “Leave it”
Confirm → “Do it”

--------------------------------
F. LOADING STATES

Add subtle text:
- “Sorting it…”
- “Working it out…”
- “Putting a week together…”

--------------------------------
8. FINAL PRODUCT GOAL
--------------------------------

The app should feel like:

- calm
- quietly capable
- lightly witty
- not demanding
- not overwhelming

The user should feel:

“Okay. That’s manageable.”

And:

“Nothing urgent. I can leave it there.”
