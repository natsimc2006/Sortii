Refresh the visual styling of my mobile-first meal planning app “Sortii: Dinner… Sorted.” to feel fresher, cleaner, lighter, and more appetising.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT redesign the app from scratch.
- Do NOT change the core structure, navigation, or information architecture.
- Keep the existing Sortii brand colours and overall identity.
- This is a visual refinement and polish pass only.
- Goal: shift the app from slightly muted/soft beige to a fresher white-based interface that makes the teal, gold, food imagery, and content feel more vibrant and modern.

OVERALL VISUAL DIRECTION:
I want the app to feel:
- fresh
- clean
- easy
- modern
- bright
- lightly premium
- warm, not sterile
- organised, but not corporate
- helpful and friendly, with a subtle cheeky personality

Reference feeling:
- bright white or softly warm-white background
- crisp card separation
- more contrast
- food imagery feels more hero
- accent colours pop more clearly
- UI feels airy and breathable

--------------------------------------------------
1. GLOBAL COLOUR + SURFACE CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------

Update the UI so that white becomes the main canvas.

Use this surface hierarchy:
- Primary app background: white or very soft warm white
  - preferred: #FFFFFF
  - acceptable: #FAFAF8 or #FCFBF8
- Secondary sections: very subtle tinted off-white only where needed
- Cards: white
- Borders: very soft warm grey or pale stone
- Avoid large flat beige/grey backgrounds that dull the interface

Keep the existing brand accents:
- teal as primary brand action/accent
- golden yellow as reward/highlight/CTA accent
- plum as personality/secondary accent
- charcoal / near-black for headings
- soft muted neutrals for borders and background support

Suggested visual usage:
- Teal = selected states, active nav, key labels, icons, progress accents
- Gold = floating action button, major CTA, celebratory highlights
- Plum = supportive notes, secondary banners, subtle personality moments
- Neutral beige = only as a very soft supporting tone, not the main page background

Improve overall contrast:
- headings darker
- body text slightly darker than current
- selected buttons clearer
- outlines cleaner
- surfaces more distinct

--------------------------------------------------
2. TYPOGRAPHY + VISUAL CLARITY
--------------------------------------------------

Keep current font family unless already deeply embedded, but improve hierarchy and clarity:
- Main headings: darker, clearer, slightly more premium feeling
- Body text: medium-dark neutral for better readability
- Secondary helper text: softened but still readable
- Small labels should not feel washed out

Tone:
- calm confidence
- easy to scan
- not overly playful
- not too serious

Increase visual hierarchy by making:
- page titles more dominant
- section labels clearer
- helper text more intentionally secondary
- selected options visually more obvious

--------------------------------------------------
3. SPACING + AIRINESS
--------------------------------------------------

Increase the sense of breathing room throughout the app without changing layout structure.

Apply:
- slightly more vertical spacing between sections
- slightly more internal padding inside cards
- clearer separation between page sections
- ensure cards feel like they float on the page rather than blend into it

Goal:
- cleaner scan
- less crowded
- fresher visual rhythm
- more premium feel

--------------------------------------------------
4. CARD STYLING
--------------------------------------------------

Update cards across the app to feel crisp and elevated.

For cards:
- white background
- subtle border in very light neutral
- optional very soft shadow
- rounded corners retained
- avoid heavy shadow
- avoid muddy backgrounds

Cards should feel:
- clean
- layered
- easy to tap
- modern

Suggested effect:
- thin pale outline
- soft shadow with low opacity
- strong separation from page background

--------------------------------------------------
5. BUTTONS, PILLS, CHIPS, AND TOGGLES
--------------------------------------------------

Refresh all selectable controls to feel clearer and more polished.

For selected pills/buttons:
- teal fill with white text for primary selected state
- ensure selected state is obvious at a glance
- slightly stronger contrast than current

For unselected pills/buttons:
- white or very light warm-neutral fill
- thin soft outline
- dark text
- cleaner than current muted beige look

For chips and tags:
- make them feel more deliberate and colour-coded
- retain softness, but improve clarity and consistency

Do NOT make everything highly saturated.
The goal is refined freshness, not loudness.

--------------------------------------------------

- Keep the logo area clean and centred
- Increase contrast of headline and supporting text
- Allow more white space around hero copy
- Make the page feel more premium and light
- “How it works” cards should be white with subtle borders/shadow
- Icon tiles inside steps can keep colour, but should pop more on white
- Teal accents should look fresher because of the brighter canvas
- Preserve the warm, reassuring tone

- Meal cards should be white and feel elevated
- Improve hierarchy between:
  - day headings

  - keep it friendly, helpful, and noticeable

- The green/teal confirmation line (“Week sorted. You’re done here.”) should feel satisfying and crisp
- Ensure bottom action controls (“Fill Gaps”, “Save”, delete icon) feel clean and modern on white

- Preserve readability and a premium-casual recipe app feel

- Keep the form structure and sections as-is

  - selected = teal fill / white text

- Preserve the welcoming personality while improving freshness and contrast

--------------------------------------------------
7. FOOD IMAGE TREATMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Food imagery is a major emotional asset in this app.
Adjust presentation so food feels fresher and more appetising.

Guidelines:
- Allow food photos to stand out more against the brighter interface
- Avoid surrounding them with dull background tones
- Use white space and clean card framing to make food the emotional focal point
- Keep images warm, inviting, and vibrant

--------------------------------------------------
8. NAVIGATION + ICON TREATMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Keep the existing navigation structure and icon system, but visually refine it:
- active icon state should feel crisp and branded
- inactive icons should be softer but still legible
- bottom navigation should feel clean against a white base
- ensure floating center action remains visually strong and intentional

--------------------------------------------------
9. SHADOWS, BORDERS, AND ELEVATION
--------------------------------------------------

Use a restrained design system:
- very light borders
- subtle shadows only where needed
- rounded corners remain
- avoid heavy depth or glossy effects

Aim for:
- soft modern polish
- card clarity
- layered but minimal feel

--------------------------------------------------
10. WHAT NOT TO CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------

Do NOT:
- change the app’s core navigation
- change the feature flow
- change the brand name, logo, or major brand colours
- redesign content structure
- remove the warmth/personality
- make the interface feel sterile, clinical, or overly techy
- overuse beige backgrounds
- overuse shadows
- make it look corporate

--------------------------------------------------
11. FINAL DESIGN GOAL
--------------------------------------------------

The end result should feel like:
“Sortii now looks brighter, fresher, and more premium — while still feeling warm, practical, friendly, and easy.”

It should feel like:
- less visual heaviness
- more freshness
- more appetite appeal
- clearer interaction states
- cleaner white-based surfaces
- a nicer balance between function and lifestyle appeal

Please update shared styles / theme tokens first where possible, then apply the changes consistently across:
- landing / intro page
- planner page
- shopping list page
- recipe detail page
- preferences / quick setup page

If possible, centralise:
- page background colours
- card background colours
- border colours
- shadow styles
- text colours
- selected / unselected pill styles
- CTA styles

So the refresh is consistent across the app.
