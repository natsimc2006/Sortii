### 1b. Rewrite the hero card — clarify what the number means

The hero card currently has line items ("Smarter ingredient choices", 
"Pantry-friendly meal selection") inside it. This creates ambiguity about 
whether the headline figure is already saved or could be saved. Fix this.

Hero card layout (teal tint background #E6F3F0):

Top row:
- Left: "THIS WEEK" label (10px, uppercase, teal #2A8F88)
- Right: status pill — "On track" / "Tight week" / "Holding steady" 
  (teal #2A8F88 background, white text, small rounded pill)

Hero number:
- "~$[totalSorted]" — large (36px), bold, teal (#2A8F88)
- Label directly below the number: "saved this week vs. eating out"
- This figure represents the meal plan cost saving compared to the user's 
  estimated usual spend (eating out / unplanned dinners). It is NOT the 
  potential saving from tips.

Remove from inside the hero card:
- The "Smarter ingredient choices — $X" line item
- The "Pantry-friendly meal selection — $X" line item
- The italic disclaimer copy inside the card

Add below the hero card (outside, not inside):
- Single line in small grey text (12px, #999999):
  "Estimated saving vs. your usual spend. Not exact — close enough."

Safety rules:
- If totalSorted is 0, null, or undefined: suppress the hero card entirely
- Never render ~$0 or ~$NaN

- Copy: "One streaming subscription you're not using — easy to pause."
