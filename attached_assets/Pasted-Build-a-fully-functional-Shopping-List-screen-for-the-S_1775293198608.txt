Build a fully functional Shopping List screen for the Sortii app.
This is a new screen that sits in the bottom navigation bar.

Use the existing Sortii brand colours throughout:
- Background: #F7F3EB
- Primary text: #2B2B2B
- Teal (standard actions): #2A8F88
- Gold (primary CTA): #D29A22
- Border: #E6E1D7
- Destructive: #B86557
- Destructive tint: #F7E9E8
- Teal tint: #E6F3F0

─── SECTION 1: Auto-generated meal ingredients ───

Pull all ingredients from the current week's planned meals in 
PlannerContext.

Consolidate duplicate ingredients intelligently:
- Same ingredient across multiple recipes = combined into one line
- Show quantity and unit where available
- Example: "Chicken thigh — 800g" not two separate "400g" entries

Group ingredients into these categories in this order:
  🥩 Meat & Seafood
  🥛 Dairy & Eggs  
  🥦 Fruit & Veg
  🥫 Pantry & Tins
  🌿 Herbs & Spices
  🍞 Bread & Bakery
  Other

Use simple keyword matching to assign categories. Examples:
- chicken, beef, lamb, pork, mince, fish, salmon, prawn, bacon → Meat
- milk, cream, cheese, butter, egg, yoghurt → Dairy & Eggs
- onion, garlic, tomato, carrot, potato, spinach, lemon, apple → Fruit & Veg
- pasta, rice, flour, oil, stock, canned, tin, sauce → Pantry & Tins
- basil, coriander, thyme, cumin, paprika, herb, spice → Herbs & Spices
- bread, wrap, pita, tortilla → Bread & Bakery

─── SECTION 2: User interactions on each item ───

Each ingredient row has:
- A checkbox on the left (tap to strike-through — "already have it")
- The ingredient name and quantity
- A quantity adjustment control: [ − ] [qty] [ + ]
- A small delete button (×) on the right using #B86557

Checked/struck-through items move to a collapsed "Already have it" 
section at the bottom, not deleted — user can uncheck to restore.

─── SECTION 3: Add your own items ───

Below the meal ingredients, a clearly separated section titled 
"Add your own" with Sortii copy: "Toilet paper, snacks, whatever."

A simple text input with a teal [ + Add ] button.
User types item name, taps Add, item appears in an "Extras" list.
Extras items also have the checkbox and delete (×) controls.
No category assignment needed for extras — they sit in their own group.

─── SECTION 4: Share / Export ───

A sticky footer with a gold primary button: "Share list"

Tapping "Share list" opens a bottom sheet (modal) with two options:

Option 1 — "Open in Notes"
  Formats the full list as plain text:
  
  SORTII SHOPPING LIST
  Week of [current date]
  
  MEAT & SEAFOOD
  • Chicken thigh — 800g
  • Beef mince — 500g
  
  DAIRY & EGGS
  • Eggs — 6
  
  [etc by category]
  
  EXTRAS
  • Toilet paper
  
  ————————————
  Generated by Sortii
  
  Uses Linking.openURL('notes://') to open Apple Notes.
  The text is copied to clipboard first so user can paste it in.
  Show a small toast: "Copied! Paste into Notes."

Option 2 — "Send to myself"
  Opens a second choice: Email or SMS
  
  Email: uses Linking.openURL('mailto:?subject=Sortii Shopping List&body=[encoded list text]')
  SMS: uses Linking.openURL('sms:?body=[encoded list text]')

─── SECTION 5: Header ───

Screen header shows:
- Title: "Shopping List"
- Subtitle: "[X] items · Est. $[rangeLow]–$[rangeHigh]" 
  (pull the cost range from PlannerContext)
- A small teal "Clear checked" button top right that removes all 
  struck-through items permanently (with a confirmation: 
  "Remove [X] checked items?" Yes / Cancel)

─── SECTION 6: Empty state ───

If no meals are planned yet, show a centred empty state:
- Icon: 🛒
- Heading: "Nothing here yet."
- Subtext: "Plan your week first, then your list builds itself."
- Teal button: "Go to Planner"
